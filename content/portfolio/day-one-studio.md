Long before the PanelFRAK website, I built a site for my former employer, **Day One Studio**. Development was underway when disagreements on another topic led to my departure. The live site was deleted, but the wireframe remains.

## Direction

- Dark studio aesthetic with Indonesian comics positioning (“Underline” hero treatment, Webtoon CTA)
- Navigation for Home, Tentang, Karya, Blog, Kontak, plus store entry
- Welcome copy framing Day One’s creative world and comic slate

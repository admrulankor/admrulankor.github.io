Every good system starts with a handshake. This is mine.

This site is a dual-purpose outpost:

1. **Portfolio** — project files, demos, and the story behind selected builds.
2. **Blog** — transmissions about engineering, design, and the tools that keep the lights on.

## Why sci-fi?

Because interfaces should feel intentional. Grid lines, orange signal glow, and typography that could belong on a starship console — all of that is a reminder to treat every page as a designed surface, not a dump of content.

## What to expect next

I'll share project write-ups, notes on Hugo and modern CSS, and the occasional field report from experiments that almost worked (and a few that did).

If you're reading this, the uplink is live. Welcome aboard.
